# Setting up SSO in Beacon 4.2 (synthetic)

This guide walks you through single sign-on, step by step, with screenshots for every screen.
Go to Settings, then Security, then SAML. Paste your identity provider metadata URL and click Save.
SSO applies immediately to all users, and local passwords keep working as a fallback.
