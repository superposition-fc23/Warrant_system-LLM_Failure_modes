01

Vision

Enterprises will run agents as colleagues. The ones that scale will be the ones whose agents can disagree safely: the dissent is kept, evidence decides, and a person is asked only when it matters.

What I believeWhat it implies for the product
Agents are becoming first-class workers with identities and permissions (Entra Agent ID, Workday Agent System of Record)Conflicts between agents become an operating problem, not a research curiosity
The scarce resource is the person with authority, not computeSell throughput: more decisions act safely without them
Convergence should be a state update, not a longer conversationRead the evidence behind an action; never add another debate loop
Reliability is earned in a loop, not shipped in a releaseIntervene at inference, evaluate in shadow, prove improvement over time

The direction I am committing to, before the solution

A closed reliability loop for multi-agent workflows, at three time scales, built from today's agents and LLMs:

LoopWhenWhat it doesParallel in Dyssonance's thesis
1. InterveneAt inferenceBefore a high-impact write, check the evidence: contested by another agent or a newer source, or stale. Return act, gather or holdWhether the system "knows enough to act"; dissonance and decay
2. LearnDuring and after the runTrace every run; judges score in shadow without acting; offline replay of traces and planted defects; human rulings become eval casesBelief revision as evidence arrives
3. ProveOver weeksOnline evals on live runs, benchmarked against the past; regressions trip the kill switchTransitions between states as learning signals
Why build the harness-era version on purpose

Loops 1 to 3 are the best version possible with today's tools. The deliverable is the evidence: what the loop costs, where it still fails, and a replacement map of which parts a persistent belief state would subsume, improve, or leave untouched. It need not be their architecture to test their hypothesis.

02

The failure mode

What breaks, stated as an observable behaviour, with no solution in the sentence.

An agent commits an action on a belief that is contested or stale, and nothing records what it relied on. The most visible and fastest-growing source of contest is other agents: when two specialists reach different conclusions about the same object, today's systems settle it by mechanics (last write wins, the most confident agent acts, or the coordinator picks), and the action executes against the system of record.

Evidence: Spawn, an agent-native company landing about 108 changes an hour, resolves agent conflicts by letting whoever lands second reconcile, and keeps no record of the competing positions (Spawn CEO blog, 28 Oct 2026, pp. 3 to 6). Academic failure taxonomies find inter-agent misalignment is a large share of multi-agent failures, though on research coding and maths tasks (MAST, arXiv 2503.13657). The enterprise rate is unmeasured; that is the first thing to find out.

How I chose

Frequent enough to matter, expensive when it happens, invisible to the person it hurts until too late, and containable by a product rather than only by a better model. A better model in each agent still disagrees with a better model in the next one; conflicting evidence is not a capability gap.

03

The business problem

The same failure in the language of the person paying for it.

Operations owners see agent actions reversed, refunds clawed back, customers told the wrong status, and a growing queue of escalations they cannot clear.

Each one costs handle time and rework, and in regulated workflows a wrong irreversible action becomes a loss or a finding. The per-incident cost is unmeasured assumed material; the log pull in section 06 replaces this line with a number.

VerticalThe conflictThe rule that existsWhere the rule fails
Retail bankingService agent sends a cheque book to a just-changed address while the transaction agent holds an open dispute and KYC is staleAccount-takeover rule blocks address change plus cheque bookFires on every genuine move; cannot see the KYC refresh completed an hour ago elsewhere
FreightException agent tells the customer a container is released while the broker shows an exam holdCustoms status outranks carrierCannot express that the hold is six hours old, or the exam finished but was not posted
CRM serviceRefund agent approves a credit while risk has flagged the account and the contract overrides termsRisk outranks serviceCannot tell the flag was cleared yesterday or the contract was amended

Enterprise structure makes it harder: RBAC means the agent that can act is often not the one that knows; hierarchy means some views outrank others only while current; PII rules limit what an escalation may show to whom.

Why now

Organisations orchestrating multiple agents rose from 9% to 18% in one quarter (KPMG AI Pulse, to verify). Gartner expects over 40% of agentic AI projects cancelled by end 2027, partly over risk controls. Agents now get directory identities and HR records (Entra Agent ID; Workday Agent System of Record). Spawn and Block show where action volume is heading; they are colour, not proof of enterprise pain.

The real threat, and the wedge

Platforms will build the in-estate version. The wedge is what they structurally cannot see: high-impact writes whose evidence spans two or more systems of record, such as KYC in core banking, disputes in the card platform and service in the CRM.

Is the Spawn and Block direction a judgement call or a trend chase?

Partly a trend chase, and I am treating it that way. Spawn is ten people writing code, and Block's restructuring is about one agent, not agents disagreeing. They are used as colour for where action volume goes, never as evidence. The evidence has to come from five operations-owner interviews and one log pull counting reversals and overrides, before design is committed.

Constraints carried into design

Bounded output (act, gather, hold) from a small classifier, costed per resolved conflict, not per token. Escalate only real disagreements: users approve about 93% of Claude Code permission prompts (Anthropic, as cited by C. Lozano). Hold is a first-class output and is bias-tested with counterfactual pairs, because KillBench found structured outputs collapse refusals and amplify bias (White Circle). Escalations are reviewable artifacts in the surface the person already uses, ruled on by comment, the pattern Antigravity uses to supervise parallel agents; Antigravity's artifacts are per agent, and nothing adjudicates across agents touching the same object. Evaluate on planted defects in our own workflow, not a leaderboard.

Four numbers I would set deliberately rather than inherit

The confidence cutoff for acting alone, the accuracy target for the decision itself, the error-rate limit and its window, and how quickly a human has to be told. I would set them from the cost of being wrong in each direction, and I would want the first version deliberately too conservative.
