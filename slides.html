Warrant in three lines

The problem
Support, success and retention agents now issue credits, refunds and waivers on evidence that can be out of date or contradicted by another system. At enterprises early in agentic adoption, the mistake can surface as late as month-end close.
The solution
Warrant sits in front of every money action. It reads the systems of record, weighs how current and consistent the evidence is, and returns act, gather or hold, with one question for the person who owns the fact.
Value and impact
Agents act alone when the evidence agrees, and people see only the real exceptions. On 224 generated test cases it stopped all 120 defects while sending about 7% of clean cases to a person on average (9 of 104 in the worst run).

Why it happens

Each system is right about its own record. The order form, the amendment, the billing list and the risk flag live in different places, and no single check reads them together.

When agents disagree, mechanics settle it. The last write wins, or the most confident agent acts, and nobody records the view that lost.

Why now: the share of firms orchestrating multiple agents doubled in one quarter, from 9% to 18%.

KPMG Q2 2026 AI Pulse, 204 US leaders at firms above $1B revenue. Spawn CEO blog, 28 Oct 2026. How often this happens is unmeasured

Built for the two problems below. Its results follow the demo.

Ceiling one: the billing lag

A freshness window can only say "this record is recent". It cannot say "this list lags its own writes by over a minute". A freshly read list looked complete, and it was wrong.

The problemThe productThe proofJudgment and next steps

"Isn't this just a guardrail?"

A guardrail says yes or no. The belief layer also says how sure it is, and which fact would settle it.

Age, not a cliff

Evidence keeps its weight while fresh and fades near the old window, instead of dropping from trusted to useless at one hour mark.

Silence must be earned

Each check the model reports cites a record, and code confirms that record exists. Nothing found is a claim, not a default.

Disagreement is evidence

When the answer could change the decision, the model runs three times. One dissent lowers confidence instead of being thrown away.

Expected loss decides

Chance of being wrong times the amount, against the cost of waiting: act, re-read or hold. Hard vetoes still win every time.

P(safe) assumes independent facts. All priors are assumed and were fixed before the runs. The unread-fact value of 0.8 is a policy, not a measurement
9:30 to 10:30. A few pure functions, no new dependencies. The model stays an extractor, because its outputs are not calibrated probabilities. A cited ID shows the record exists, not that the model read it; say this limit yourself. If asked what is Bayesian: each fact's belief combines a starting assumption with fresh evidence, rulings update how much each source is trusted, and the decision weighs the chance of being wrong against the cost of waiting.

Appendix

What it must not do

  • Never act on a failed read. Timeout, error or malformed reply gathers. Tested
  • Never move money without an act on that exact proposal, re-read before commit. Tested

  • Never apply a rule outside its scope or past its expiry. Tested
  • N3, kill switch: reversal rate 1 point above baseline over 7 days, or one reversal the gate should have caught, reverts that action type to approval and tells the VP and engineer within 15 minutes assumed Not built
