2 · The failure, and who pays

Agents act on beliefs nobody checked

Support, success and retention agents commit money on evidence that conflicts or is out of date.

  • Illustration: a retention agent credits an account that risk flagged an hour ago; a second agent then credits the same incident
  • Conflicts settle by mechanics: last write wins, or the most confident agent acts. Spawn lets whoever lands second reconcile, and keeps no record of the competing positions
  • Why now: firms orchestrating multiple agents doubled in a quarter, 9% to 18% (KPMG)
  • Support engineer: reversed credits and reopened tickets, never the conflict behind them
  • VP of Support: automation rises with no view of the clawbacks against it
  • Finance: at enterprises early in agentic adoption, finds out at month-end close, and sees the total, not which agent decision caused it
  • Size: TAM $3.0B, SOM $25M ARR by year 3 assumed. Rate and cost per incident are unmeasured
Spawn CEO blog, 28 Oct 2026; MAST, arXiv 2503.13657 (research tasks); KPMG Q2 2026 AI Pulse, 204 US leaders, firms above $1B. Finance: one practitioner's view
1:00 to 3:00. Why this failure over the others: frequent, expensive, invisible until late, and a product can contain it. A better model does not fix it, because the evidence itself disagrees. Say once: "I built it on support credits; the same gate sits in front of any customer-facing agent that moves money." Lead with throughput, not fear: more decisions act safely without a person. Sizing is bottom-up and assumed: 12,000 multi-agent enterprises at $100k to $500k a year; SOM is 100 accounts at $250k. Say assumed out loud. The month-end point is a hypothesis: firms with approval thresholds and QA sampling catch some errors sooner.

6 · The belief layer

"Isn't this just a guardrail?"

A guardrail says yes or no. This also says how sure it is, and what would settle it.

  • Age, not a cliff: evidence fades as it gets old
  • Silence must be earned: each check cites a record, and code confirms it exists
  • Disagreement is evidence: three samples when the answer can change the decision
  • Expected loss against cost of delay: act, re-read or hold. Hard vetoes still win
P(safe) assumes independent facts. All priors assumed. The unread-fact 0.8 is a policy, not a measurement
8:00 to 9:30. A few pure functions, no new dependencies. The model stays an extractor, because its outputs are not calibrated probabilities. A cited ID shows the record exists, not that the model read it. Say this limit yourself.

8 · Where the AI was wrong

Every eval passed. Then the live agent double-credited a customer

Chargebee's credit list lagged its own write by over a minute. An empty list looks exactly like "no prior credit".

  • Why evals missed it: a fixture is always current, so this failure could not appear in any test
  • The fix was not a better model: the gate keeps a ledger of its commits, and stops when the balance exceeds listed credits
  • The thesis applies to the gate too: a belief can be well formed, freshly read and wrong
Logged in product/llm-failure-modes-log.md, with 34 other failures from this build

A second one in the final live check: a Commit click wrote a second credit, because the card named nothing to weigh. Reading the stored raw answers caught it.

10 · The dial

At a $20 cost of delay, a person sees 7% of clean cases and no defect acts

  • Below $20: no defect acts, but a person sees up to 73% of clean cases
  • Above $20: it lets defects through and saves no review
  • Review time alone doesn't pay: about $1,170 to $1,570 a month at 5,000 cases assumed. v3 was at about 6% too, so v4 raised catches, not savings
  • The case rests on what is not counted: clawbacks, lost trust and manual account management. None is measured yet
Stored beliefs replayed at each cost, no new model calls. Share excludes timed re-reads. Assumes 5% of cases are defects, all seen by a person. Cost table in backup

Backup · Guardrails

What it must not do

  • Never act on a failed read. Timeout, error or malformed reply gathers. Tested
  • Never move money without an act on that exact proposal, re-read before commit. Tested
  • Never settle a disagreement silently. The losing belief stays on the record. Built
  • Never apply a rule outside its scope or past its expiry. Tested
  • N3, kill switch: reversal rate 1 point above baseline over 7 days, or one reversal the gate should have caught, reverts that action type to approval and tells the VP and engineer within 15 minutes assumed Not built
